# Idea Tools

Pick one or two that suit the moment. Do not run through them all.

- **Collide:** combine two unrelated things, such as a genre and a daily job, then find the mechanic the mix creates.
- **Flip an assumption:** take a genre rule and reverse it. The player is the boss, healing hurts, or enemies are the map.
- **One verb:** choose one verb, such as push, trade, or hide, and build the whole game around doing it in deeper ways.
- **Add a harsh limit:** one button, one room, ten seconds, no attacks. Limits force fresh solutions.
- **Start from the feeling:** recall a real-life moment, such as a narrow escape, and ask what rules would recreate it.
- **Steal from outside games:** borrow a structure from sports, cooking, board games, or nature.
- **Raise the stakes:** ask what happens if the core action costs something the player cares about.
- **Change the perspective:** tell the same game from the villain, a shopkeeper, or a side character.
- **Make the theme do work:** change a theme until it alters the rules. If swapping the theme changes nothing, the theme is only paint.
- **Kill the first ideas:** discard the first three that come to mind, since they are the ones everyone gets.
